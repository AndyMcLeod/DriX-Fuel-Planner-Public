    Survey arrival is the start of the first survey leg and departure the end of the last, so a reposition between patches sits inside time on task rather than splitting it. Distance-from-home marks are measured along the planned track: run made good on the first leg, distance still to run on the last. The planner has no position model, so those are the only two honest readings of “distance from home”.

    The tank is 250 L, and the gauge is treated as non-linear

    The engineering drawings settle the physical volume at 250 L on every hull, while the measured band says 2.06 L per indicated point — which only fit together if the gauge reads differently in bands nobody has measured. The planner adopts that reading (A): the 72–86% band stays at the measured rate and the other 86 points carry the balance, at about 2.57. This is an inference, not a measurement. On the conservative reading (B) the gauge speaks only for its own ~206 L span and mission fuel drops from 211 L to 175 L. One drawdown to the floor separates them.
